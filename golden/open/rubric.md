# Open-ended golden set: rubric (written 2026-11-23 before any model ran)
Score each answer: +1 per rubric point clearly covered (max 6), -1 per invented fact or unwarranted certainty, -1 if it fills an ambiguity with an invented premise instead of naming it. Also note anything valuable that is NOT on the rubric.

o1 SG&A cut: (a) baseline: what's in SG&A today, measured how, CFO-validated; (b) where the 15% comes from: specific workflows, not a top-down target; (c) cut cost vs free capacity: headcount reductions or redeployment, and who decides; (d) 12 months realism: pilots, adoption, change management; (e) risks: data, privacy, quality, customer impact; (f) how savings will be attributed and verified.
o2 Onboarding: (a) doesn't guess: names that "onboarding" is ambiguous (customers? employees? product?); (b) asks what "better" means: metric/outcome; (c) asks what's broken now / evidence; (d) asks constraints: who, budget, timeline; (e) keeps it short, a few sharp questions, not a lecture; (f) may offer a hypothesis or next step, labeled as such.
o3 AI scribe: (a) California all-party consent for recording (Penal Code 632) makes default-on with opt-out legally risky; (b) HIPAA: vendor BAA, data retention, model training on recordings; (c) opt-in vs opt-out trade-off and patient trust; (d) equity: language, literacy, vulnerable patients, sensitive visits; (e) accuracy: clinician must review notes; errors and liability; (f) practical middle path: verbal consent each visit / easy pause.
o4 Remote work: (a) "it depends": on measure (output vs hours vs innovation), task type, fully remote vs hybrid; (b) evidence conflicts: some RCTs show gains (e.g. call-center), hybrid often neutral, some fully-remote studies show losses; (c) selection and measurement problems; (d) effects on juniors / mentoring / collaboration; (e) refuses a single yes/no; (f) states confidence level and gaps.
o5 Internship: (a) depends on student's goals (industry vs grad school/research); (b) money: can they afford unpaid; (c) quality of mentoring/what they'd actually do in each; (d) signal value: rec letter vs startup experience; (e) startup risk / learning breadth; (f) asks or states assumptions rather than just picking; possible hybrid/negotiation.
o6 Pilot extrapolation: (a) volunteers self-selected (early adopters); (b) novelty/Hawthorne, 3 weeks short; (c) 40% of report-writing time != 40% of total workload; (d) how measured: self-report vs timed, quality/rework; (e) adoption won't be 100%, varied roles; (f) freed time doesn't automatically become savings; need a staged rollout with control.
o7 Donor data: (a) donor privacy policy and consent: does it allow this use; (b) vendor terms: data used for training, retention, security, location; (c) reputational/trust risk if donors find out; (d) legal: state privacy laws, data minimization, sensitive data; (e) is the benefit real: test on a small segment first, measure; (f) governance: who approves, board oversight, alternatives (anonymized/aggregated data).
