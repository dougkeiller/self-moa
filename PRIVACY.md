# Privacy policy: self-moa

Last updated: 2026-11-24

self-moa is an open-source plugin of instructions for Claude. The author runs no server and no service for it, and receives, collects, stores and shares no data from anyone who uses it.

It sends your question to additional Claude subagents in the same session; nothing leaves your Claude account.

Anything you send to Claude is handled under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy, not by this plugin's author.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dougkeiller/self-moa/issues.
